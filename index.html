Air Pen+ controls (H to hide)

Pinch
Touch thumb and index finger together to draw. Open them to stop.
Shift
Hold to draw without pinching (same pointer).
Space
Clear everything.
Z
Undo the last stroke (also restores a cleared drawing).
C
Change colour.
[ and ]
Thinner or thicker brush.
K
Calibrate to the projector: pinch and hold on each of 4 dots.
R
Reset calibration.
M
Flip left and right (when not calibrated).
V
Show or hide the camera preview.
P
Show or hide speed stats (fps, detect and draw time).
F
Fullscreen on or off.
